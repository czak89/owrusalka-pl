A quick reference for the brand voice: warm, host-like, concrete. The full rules are in the brand book under "Content fundamentals".

## Rules

- **Do:** formal plural in Polish (*Państwo*, *zapraszamy*), concrete detail („50 metrów do plaży"), a light touch of nostalgia.
- **Don't:** corporate puff („premium SPA experience"), emoji in body copy or buttons, all-caps shouting or strings of exclamation marks.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons. ALL CAPS only for tiny eyebrow labels (14px and under).
