Three decorative motifs that echo the logo: the wave band, the sun disc and the organic blob. They are illustration, not icons, so they do not sit in tight UI lockups.

**You provide** the shape as inline SVG or CSS; there is no script.

## Rules

- **Wave band:** two overlapping waves, `c-baltic-blue` under `c-deep-sea`, on a `c-light-sky` to `c-paper` wash. Footers, section dividers.
- **Sun disc:** radial gradient `#FFEE85` to `c-sun-yellow` to `c-amber`, with a soft `c-sun-yellow` halo. The two pale tints (`#FFEE85`, `#FFE9A8`) belong to this motif only and are not tokens.
- **Organic blob:** an image mask or a flat `c-coral` shape. Use it for photos more often than a rectangle.
- One motif per composition. Idle drift, if any, is slow: a 4 to 6 second loop, about 2px.
